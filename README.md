# Hi, I'm Lucas Casarim 👋

### Software Engineer @ Uber | Enterprise Identity & Administration

I’m a Software Engineer focused on building reliable, scalable software and solving complex problems through strong engineering fundamentals, system design, and pragmatic execution.

At **Uber**, I work on enterprise identity and administration platforms used by internal Operations teams and enterprise customers across business lines such as **Uber Direct, Merchant/Eats, Ads, Uber for Business, and Supplier Fleets**.

My work spans **frontend and full-stack engineering**, including React/TypeScript applications, GraphQL/API integrations, asynchronous workflows, RBAC and authorization, automated testing, performance optimization, observability, and technical design.
